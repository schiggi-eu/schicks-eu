G1 · Modalverben & ihre Ersatzformen

Modals and their substitute forms – Die Ersatzformen der Modalverben

Die Grundregel

Die Modalverben can, can't, may, must, mustn't und needn't gibt es nur im simple present.
Für alle anderen Zeiten (simple past, present perfect, will future …) brauchst du eine Ersatzform.

1. Erlaubnis / Verbot → be allowed to

ModalverbSimple presentSimple past
can / may
dürfen		Everyone is allowed to choose their own project.The groups were allowed to discuss different ideas.
can't / mustn't
nicht dürfen		We aren't allowed to choose our groups.We weren't allowed to go out at night.
FrageAm I allowed to use my phone?Were you allowed to work with other groups?

2. Fähigkeit → be able to

ModalverbSimple presentSimple past
can
können		Rishi and Lauren are able to go home at night.The participants were able to come up with lots of ideas.
can't
nicht können		One group isn't able to stay polite.Ollie's group wasn't able to choose a leader.

Für eine allgemeine Fähigkeit in der Vergangenheit geht auch could / couldn't (When I was five, I could swim.). Bei einem einmaligen Erfolg nimmt man was/were able to (The participants were able to come up with lots of ideas.). In Aufgaben zu den Ersatzformen immer die Ersatzform benutzen!

3. Notwendigkeit → have to

ModalverbSimple presentSimple past
must
müssen		Rishi doesn't understand why he has to be at the workshop.Last year, Jess had to stop a group from arguing.
needn't
nicht müssen		Jess doesn't have to help the groups.Ollie didn't have to say a lot.
FrageDo we have to bring pens?Did you have to sign the rules?
⚠ Achtung, Falle!

mustn't = nicht dürfen  → You mustn't eat in the workshop space. (Verbot)
needn't / don't have to = nicht müssen  → You needn't / don't have to bring anything. (nicht nötig)

Merksatz: „must not“ klingt wie „muss nicht“, heißt aber „darf nicht“!

4. Zukunft (will future)

Next year, Ollie will be able to help again.
We won't be allowed to use our phones in the sessions.
You will have to present your project.

5. Fragen & Verneinung – so geht's

be allowed to / be able to Hier arbeitest du mit der Form von be:
Are you allowed to …? · Was he able to …?
I'm not allowed to … · They weren't able to …
have to Hier brauchst du do / does / did:
Do you have to …? · Did she have to …?
He doesn't have to … · We didn't have to …

6. „can“ hat zwei Bedeutungen

You can ask me anything. → Erlaubnis = be allowed to
You're going to think about how you can make some positive change. → Fähigkeit = be able to

Überlege bei can immer zuerst: Geht es ums Dürfen oder ums Können? Danach wählst du die Ersatzform.
