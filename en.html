Profile

A mathematician in energy trading

I come from mathematics: a PhD in stochastic analysis at Swansea University, followed by two and a half years of teaching and research in stochastics at TU Braunschweig.

In 2010 I moved into gas and spent more than a decade at RWE and Uniper – first in commodity risk management, then structuring and pricing storage and flexible supply contracts, and as Commercial Manager Gas Storage.

Today I combine interim assignments with my role as co-founder and Managing Director of Qmesa in Gdańsk, where we apply the same quantitative approach to power markets, flexibility and grid constraints.
