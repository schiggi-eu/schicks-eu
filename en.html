Profile

Mathematician. Structurer. Entrepreneur.

I am Dr. Markus Schicks and I come from mathematics: a PhD in stochastic analysis at Swansea University, followed by teaching and research in stochastics at TU Braunschweig.

In 2010 I moved into the energy industry and spent more than a decade at RWE and Uniper – in commodity risk management, as a Quantitative Structurer at RWE Supply & Trading and as Commercial Manager Gas Storage. Along the way I valued, negotiated and closed asset contracts – storage, flexibility and supply – in 11 European countries.

Today I am co-founder and Managing Director of Qmesa in Gdańsk. With our analytics platform Energy Workbench we quantify flexibility, grid constraints and risk in Germany and Poland. Through Dr. Markus Schicks Consulting I also take on selected mandates for demanding valuation and risk questions – as an interim manager where needed.
